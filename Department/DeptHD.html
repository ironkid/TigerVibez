    • Department of Human Development

    The Department of Human Development focuses on the study of how people grow and change throughout their lives—physically, emotionally, socially, and cognitively. It often includes areas like child development, family studies, aging, and education. Students learn about human behavior, relationships, and the factors that influence development across the lifespan, preparing for careers in education, counseling, social work, and health services.
