    • Department of Art

    A Department of Art is an academic division within a school, college, or university that focuses on the study and practice of the visual arts. It typically offers courses and degrees in areas like painting, sculpture, drawing, photography, digital media, and art history. The department supports students in developing creative skills, critical thinking, and an understanding of art’s cultural and historical contexts. It often includes studios, galleries, and faculty who are practicing artists or scholars.
