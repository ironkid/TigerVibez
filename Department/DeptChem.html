    The Department of Chemistry & Atmospheric Science is an academic division that combines the study of chemical principles with the science of Earth’s atmosphere. It typically offers courses and research opportunities in areas such as organic and inorganic chemistry, physical chemistry, environmental chemistry, climate science, and air quality. Students learn how chemical processes affect the environment and atmosphere, preparing them for careers in research, education, industry, or environmental policy.
