    • Department of Civil Engineering

    The Department of Civil Engineering focuses on the design, construction, and maintenance of infrastructure such as buildings, roads, bridges, and water systems. Students gain knowledge in structural engineering, transportation, geotechnical studies, and environmental engineering. Through hands-on projects, labs, and internships, they develop practical skills to solve real-world challenges. Graduates are prepared for careers in construction, project management, urban planning, and environmental protection.
