    • Department of English

    The Department of English is dedicated to the study of literature, language, and writing. It offers a range of courses in classic and contemporary literature, creative writing, rhetoric, linguistics, and cultural studies. Students develop strong communication, critical thinking, and analytical skills that prepare them for careers in education, writing, publishing, and many other fields. The department often hosts events like poetry readings, writing workshops, and guest lectures to enrich the academic experience.
