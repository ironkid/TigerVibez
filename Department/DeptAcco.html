    • Department of Accounting

    The Department of Accounting focuses on the principles and practices of financial reporting, auditing, taxation, and managerial accounting. It prepares students for careers in accounting, finance, and business by providing a strong foundation in financial analysis, regulatory compliance, and ethical decision-making. The department emphasizes analytical skills, critical thinking, and the use of modern accounting technologies to ensure graduates are well-equipped for professional roles such as Certified Public Accountant (CPA), financial analyst, or auditor. Through a combination of theoretical knowledge and practical experience, the department fosters expertise in financial management and organizational decision-making.
